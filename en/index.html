GOOD TO KNOW

A few
common questions.

Will I need new software?＋

Not necessarily. We first look at what you use now and whether those tools can connect. If a paid service is needed, we agree its costs and account ownership before starting.

What if I don’t know what to automate?＋

Tell us about a task where you move information between tools or do the same steps repeatedly. We’ll work out together whether automation makes sense.

Whose accounts will it run on?＋

Normally, your business’s own accounts. We agree access permissions, handling of personal data and any third-party services before work starts.

What happens after handover?＋

You get instructions. The single-automation package includes 30 days of fixes to the agreed work. Ongoing monitoring, maintenance and new features are agreed separately.
